Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit to Cosmic Lashes And Nails Gachibowli.

How long do acrylic nail extensions last?
↓
With proper care and maintenance, acrylic extensions last 3 to 4 weeks before needing an infill or touch-up appointment.
Are acrylic and gel nails safe for natural nails?
↓
Yes! At CLAN, we prioritize natural nail health by using non-abrasive buffing techniques and high-quality non-toxic imported gels.
How long does a lash extension session take?
↓
A full set of classic or volume lash extensions typically takes 60 to 90 minutes in our relaxing recliner chairs.
Do I need an advance appointment?
↓
While we do accept walk-ins depending on technician availability, advance booking via WhatsApp or phone is highly recommended to guarantee your preferred slot.
